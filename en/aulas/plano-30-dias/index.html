Lesson 8 · 18 min

30-day plan: one trusted service delivered

The goal of these 30 days is not a big contract. It's a small yes: someone who didn't know you pays a little for a first diagnosis, review or teaching session, you deliver on time and send a one-page report. A bigger contract is a result of the following months, not of the first one.

02 · The criteria and the numbers

Measure what you control.

1. The success criteria

On day 30 you hit the goal if you can say, without making anything up: I did the service I sell and three real people used or read it; I talked to companies in my profile; I led diagnostic calls with the person who decides; I closed a small yes and delivered on time, with a report.

"Feeling ready" doesn't count: it can't be measured and it never arrives.

2. Five numbers every Friday

Contacts made, replies, calls, offers and yeses. As an activity reference for someone with about 2 hours a day: 50 contacts, 2 calls and 2 offers per week. It's not a forecast of results; adapt to your market and your time.

Contacts and offers depend on you. Yeses depend on the client. Hold yourself accountable for the first two, celebrate the last.

3. Read the number that got stuck

Few contacts: a scheduling problem, block a fixed time. Few replies: your approach talks about you, not about their problem.

Few calls: you don't offer a time on the spot. Few offers: you're diagnosing without proposing. No yes: the first ask is still too big for the trust that exists.

4. The month 2 mistake

Closing the first client and stopping outreach. When the work ends, you're back at zero and you've lost your rhythm.

Keep your contact goal even in the week you're delivering, and turn every delivery into something you publish.

  2. 2/5

    Introduce yourself

    Requires the proof. Say who you help, what changes, what you don't do, and when you call in a specialist.

  3. 3/5

    Close

    Requires the identity. Measure the problem, ask for the smallest yes, reduce scope instead of giving a discount, and leave with a date.

    Report

    Requires the delivery. The report is the proof that stays and opens the advisory conversation.
